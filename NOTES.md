# Notes

## Clinic discovery questions
Ask these at the first pilot clinic before trusting any assumption in the code.

1. **Patients per day:** typical day? Busiest day?
2. **Doctors:** how many see walk-ins at once? One shared queue or one per doctor?
3. **Average consult time:** roughly how long per patient? Does it vary a lot (follow-ups vs. new patients)?
4. **Devices at the desk:** what phone/computer does the receptionist have? Android version? Shared or personal?
5. **Internet at the desk:** Wi-Fi or mobile data? How often does it drop? Load-shedding (power cut) schedule, and is there a UPS?
6. **How the queue runs today:** paper register? Token slips? Shouting names? What goes wrong most?
7. **Peak hours:** when does the waiting room fill up? Morning and evening shifts?
8. **Languages:** what do patients read: Urdu, English, a regional language? Literacy level?
9. (Extra) Do patients ever leave and come back? Are there priority cases (elderly, emergencies) that skip the queue?
